1 August 2026

I decided to use GameMaker

I apologize for the fact that there have been no posts lately.
In general, I went through and studied various frameworks and libraries for creating games: SDL, Pygame, Love2D MonoGame, raylib. I didn't even realize how much time was going. As a result, at the end of July, I realized that I had gone in the wrong place and decided to choose the GameMaker engine.
I would have chosen GameMaker a long time ago if it weren't for the collision issues, which were due to the crooked tutorials on YouTube, such as the 5-minute official tutorial on making a platformer in GameMaker.
I'm currently taking a video course on creating a platformer on GameMaker by Peyton Burnham on YouTube, and the picture shows what I've been able to do so far.
The very game that I promised to release at the end of the summer - I canceled. I'll be making The Semiradical on GameMaker. Of course, there will be no hanging platforms and I will try to make the slopes smoother, like in Sonic The Hedgehog.
